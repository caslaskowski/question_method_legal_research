The Question Method of Legal Research

Ask one question, find the answer, write it down, and let that answer tell you what to ask next. Repeat until no substantive question remains. The question, not the database, drives the research.

How this edition works

  • Read the chapter, then practice in it. Interactive exercises sit inside the chapters, right where the book teaches the skill. Each has an Open in a new tab link if you want more room.
  • Keep one Research Log. The interactive modules that use it share a single log in your browser, so your work follows you from chapter to chapter.
  • Your work stays with you. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Modules save to your own browser and let you download a record to submit.
  • Download the tools. The Downloads page has the Tip Sheet, the Research Log template, and a fully worked example.
