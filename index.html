Ask. Answer. Repeat.

The Question Method of Legal Research

Making the hidden curriculum of the research process visible

By Cas Laskowski

Ask one question. Find the answer. Write it down, and let that answer tell you what to ask next. Repeat until no substantive question remains. The question drives the research, whatever database happens to be open.

This edition asks you to do things as you read. Change the courtroom and watch the same authority gain or lose its force. Run a search two ways and count what each one misses. Take a paragraph apart into the questions that built it. Read with your hands on the page.
