A statute almost never answers your question in one self-contained sentence. Sort each question into the part of the annotated code that holds the answer.

::: {.try kind="sort" title="Read it in context" bins="Definitions section | Exceptions | Cross-reference | Notes of Decisions | Historical notes"}
- Does “employer” here include a business with twelve employees? **Definitions section**
    - Operative terms are usually defined elsewhere in the chapter, often near the front.
- Does the rule apply to a religious organization? **Exceptions**
    - An exception can remove your client’s conduct from the statute’s reach entirely.
- The section says the procedure in “section 2000e-5” applies; what procedure is that? **Cross-reference**
    - Incorporated sections are part of the rule. Open them.
- How have courts read “reasonable accommodation” in this provision? **Notes of Decisions**
    - Editors’ summaries of the cases construing the section: leads to verify, not answers.
- Was the fifteen-employee threshold the same in 2019, when the events occurred? **Historical notes**
    - Amendment history tells you which version was in force when. Citing today’s text for yesterday’s events is the wrong-version pitfall.
- What does “covered entity” mean in this title? **Definitions section**
    - A term of art the statute defines for itself. Never guess from ordinary usage.
- Which regulations implement this section? **Cross-reference**
    - The annotated code’s Implementing Regulations note is the pathway to the CFR (Chapter 7).
:::
