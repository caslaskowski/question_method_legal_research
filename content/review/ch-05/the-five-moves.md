Chapter 5 restates the cycle as five moves performed on a single opinion. Arrange them.

::: {.try kind="order" title="Question, read, synthesize, repeat"}
Top is the first move.

1. Bring the question to the case, keeping the question, not the case, in the foreground
    - Move 1. A secondary source, an annotation, or a search handed you the case; your current question is the lens.
2. Read for the answer, with your current question as the lens
    - Move 2. Read critically for the part that answers you; skim the rest.
3. Synthesize the answer into the annotated outline, in your own words
    - Move 3. The verified answer, not the question, goes into the outline.
4. Log the collateral questions the case answers, with its citation and the cases it cites for each
    - Move 4. You do not synthesize them now; they become pathways for later questions.
5. Choose the next question and follow the editorial tools to the cases on that point
    - Move 5. Headnotes, key numbers, and the citator connect you to related cases on the same point; keep the cycle going.
:::
