The map tells you where you are going, the log where you have been, the outline what you know. Sort each entry into the tool that should hold it.

::: {.try kind="sort" title="Where does it go?" bins="Research map | Research Log | Annotated outline"}
- A question an encyclopedia entry raised that you have not researched yet, with the case it cited as a lead **Research map**
    - The map holds the questions still to answer, each with its leads, filed where it belongs in the analysis.
- The method you used to find the source that answered your current question **Research Log**
    - The Method field. It is what lets future you avoid rerunning a search, and lets a supervisor see how you found a case.
- Your paraphrase of the Hazelwood rule, with its citation **Annotated outline**
    - A verified answer from primary authority goes into the outline, under the element it answers.
- A bracketed note that our paper is produced in a journalism class and so is almost certainly school-sponsored **Annotated outline**
    - The writing step, taken in a research pause: a bracketed application note under the element, so you can tell your analysis from your authority at a glance.
- The macro question, written once **Research Log**
    - It sits at the top of the log so you can always check that you have not drifted onto a tangent.
- A question marked parked, done for now on purpose **Research map**
    - The map tracks each question as open, answered, or parked.
- The search string that returned Kennedy v. Bremerton as a false hit, and why you set it aside **Research Log**
    - Process, including dead ends, is the log’s job. Logging the false hit saves you from re-reading it next month.
- A question about your client’s facts: who has decided what gets published? **Research map**
    - Trick. Fact questions go on a separate fact-gathering list, because no database can answer them. If you placed it on the map, the feedback is the lesson.
    - Questions about your client’s facts stay off the map; they go on a separate task list for the client.
- The next question your last answer produced **Research Log**
    - The Next Question field closes each entry; the question also lands on the map if it is not already there.
:::
