### 0.2 Method: the pause/assess/reflect structure is incomplete and described inconsistently (P1, needs-author)

- The **Quick Check** (am I in a headspace to keep going; if not, write next steps for future-you and pivot) appears nowhere in the book. `grep -i "quick check"` over content/ returns nothing. The author's checklist makes it a step in every cycle. Propose adding it to Chapter 3's cycle (after mapping, before "Choose your next question") and to the Chapter Recap and Key Takeaways.
- Chapter 3's cycle is "five steps" in the heading (line 30), "Four steps turn" in the diagram (line 57), and "question, research, synthesize, next question, repeat" in the takeaways (line 388). The author's checklist has seven moves (state, type, search, synthesize and record, map, Quick Check, choose next). Mapping is tacked onto Step 5 ("Repeat ... and add any new questions the source raised to your research map," line 40) rather than being its own move. Decide one canonical statement and use it in ch-03 (lines 30, 40, 57, 383, 388), ch-14 line 60, ch-15 line 68, ch-16 line 79.
- The **closing-out protocol** is stated three ways. Author's tip sheet: final sweep; update every authority; confirm propositions; review research map; outline gaps; name and document type of closure (six). ch-09.md lines 64 to 67: final sweep; update every case and statute; evaluate outline for gaps; document closure decision (four; "review your research map" is missing; "confirm propositions" is folded into the update bullet; the type of closure is not named). ch-03.md line 223: "a last sweep of the major sources, a citation update, and a check of the annotated outline for gaps" (three). glossary.md line 44: adds "check the currency of secondary sources," which is in none of the others. ch-09 Exercise 9.3 (line 135) adds "the check against the original question," which is in none of the others. - "Checkpoint" is used for three different things: the self-evaluation step (ch-03 lines 42, 63; ch-09 line 62), a recorded cycle ("each cycle is a recorded checkpoint," ch-03 line 225), and a false-hit note ("**Checkpoint, the false hit.**" ch-03 line 330).
