Print these. Fill them in. Keep them beside you every time you research. Everything here is free to use and to share in class.

  • The Question Method Tip Sheet

    Quick tips, the four question types with where to look for each, and a research checklist in three parts: before you start, every cycle (rinse and repeat), and checking out.

    Use it: Keep it beside you for every assignment. Run the checklist against your Research Log and research map before you call a project done.

    Download DOCX 21 KB Preview (PDF)

  • Research Log Template (spreadsheet)

    A blank log, one row per research cycle, with the book's seven fields as columns: Research Question, Question Type (a dropdown), Method, Findings, Source Consulted, Next Question, Notes.

    Use it: Open it before your first exercise, write your macro question above the table, and add a row for every cycle you run.

    Download XLSX 7 KB

  • Worked Example: Research Map, Research Log, and Annotated Outline

    One Arizona notice-of-claim problem run through all three tools, so you can see how a question moves from the map to the log to the outline.

    Use it: Read it first, then try the Notice of Claim sandbox in Chapter 3 and build your own.

    Download DOCX 18 KB Preview (PDF)

  • Key Takeaways and Common Pitfalls, by Chapter

    Every chapter's Key Takeaways and Common Pitfalls on one set of pages. Rebuilt automatically from the chapter text whenever the site is built.

    Use it: Review before an exam or a project kickoff; check that you can restate each chapter in your own words.

    Download DOCX 23 KB

  • Scholarship Research Checklist

    The ten-step method from Chapter 17 for seminar papers, notes, and comments.

    Use it: Run it when the paper is assigned, and again before submission.
