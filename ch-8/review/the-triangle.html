Chapter 8 review · Drill

The Triangle

The statute sets the framework, the regulation supplies the detail, and case law interprets both. Sort each proposition by the source that supplies it.

Which source supplies it? (Quick check)

  • Employers with fifteen or more employees may not discriminate on the basis of disability Statute
    • The framework rule and its coverage threshold come from the enacted text.
  • What a “reasonable accommodation” includes, in a list of examples Regulation
    • The agency’s implementing rule supplies the operative detail the statute delegated.
  • Whether remote work was a reasonable accommodation for a particular employee with anxiety Case law
    • Applying the rule to facts in a decided dispute is what opinions do.
  • The definition of “waters of the United States” the agency adopted Regulation
    • A term the statute delegated to the agency to define.
  • Whether that definition exceeds the agency’s statutory authority Case law
    • Courts police the boundary between the statute’s grant and the regulation’s reach.
  • The deadline for filing a charge with the agency Statute
    • Limitations periods are the legislature’s bright lines.
  • What “filing” requires in practice: the form, the office, the signature Regulation
    • Procedure is the detail agencies supply.
  • Whether an emailed charge received after hours was timely filed Case law
    • A dispute about facts against the rule, resolved by a court.
  • The agency’s authority to issue the rule at all Statute
    • Every regulation exists because a statute told an agency to write it. The authority note points back here.
